We would rather tell you this up front than have you meet it unprepared. If you would rather not click past a warning, write to us and wait for the Microsoft Store release — packages there are signed by Microsoft and install without one.

After it installs

The app opens a Setup window and walks you through these. You do not have to do anything here first — this is just so you know what is coming.

  1. Sign in. Opens your browser once. This is what your credits are attached to, and what lets your documents follow you between machines.
  2. Install the Chrome extension. Setup gives you the link. This is how heybriz works inside the browser you are already logged into — it never asks for your passwords.
  3. Paste the pairing code. Setup shows a code; the extension's popup asks for it. The two refuse to talk until you do, so nothing can reach your browser by accident.

Good to know

  • The extension needs the app running. It does nothing on its own — it is a hand, not a brain. If it says it cannot reach heybriz, start the app first.
  • It updates itself. heybriz checks for new versions and installs them in the background. You should not need to come back to this page.
  • Your documents live on this PC. They are in a heybriz folder inside your Documents folder, as ordinary files you can open without us.
