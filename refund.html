Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-21

heybriz sells credits: a prepaid balance you spend as the assistant works. This page explains when we refund them. It is part of our Terms of Service.

The short version

Unused credits are refundable within 14 days of purchase. Credits you have already spent are not, because the work they paid for has already been done.

How it works

  • Within 14 days of a top-up, write to us and we will refund the credits from that purchase that you have not spent, at the price you paid for them.
  • Credits you have already spent are not refundable. Running a task costs us money at the moment it runs, and that cost is not recoverable.
  • Free credits — the ones an account starts with — have no cash value and are never refundable.
  • Expired credits are not refundable. Credits are valid for 12 months from purchase.

If something went wrong

The 14-day rule is a floor, not a ceiling. If credits were spent because heybriz malfunctioned, was charged twice, or did something you did not ask for, tell us and we will make it right — including credits already spent, and including after 14 days. heybriz is beta software and we would rather hear about it than have you quietly stop using it.

How to request one

Email hello@heybriz.com from the address on your account, and tell us which purchase you mean. We aim to answer within three business days.

Payments are processed by Creem, our merchant of record, so an approved refund is issued by Creem and returns to your original payment method. Your bank decides how long it takes to appear — usually five to ten business days.

Closing your account

If you close your account within 14 days of a top-up, the unused credits from that purchase are refunded under this policy. If we end your account without cause, we refund your unused credits regardless of when you bought them.

Your rights

Nothing here removes a consumer protection you have where you live that cannot be waived by agreement. Where such a right gives you more than this policy does, that right applies.
