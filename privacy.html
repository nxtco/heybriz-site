Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

heybriz is a Chrome extension that operates the tab you are on, on your instruction. This policy explains what it does with your data.

What the extension sends

To work out what you asked for, the extension sends three things to heybriz: what you said or typed, the address and title of the tab you are on, and the names of the links, buttons and boxes on that page. It gets back a single action to perform.

That is the only server it talks to. The address is fixed in the extension's code — it cannot be pointed anywhere else — and there is no analytics. The one other place anything goes is Chrome's speech recognition, described below, which Chrome does rather than us.

It does not send the full text of the page, images, or anything from tabs other than the one you are working on.

What you say

heybriz uses the speech recognition built into Chrome. Chrome sends the recording to Google's speech service to turn it into text, and that service handles it under Google's own terms — this is how speech recognition works in the browser, and it is the same for any site that uses it.

heybriz never receives the audio. We only ever see the text that comes back, and only after you press the microphone. Nothing is captured while it is off.

What passes through your computer

heybriz reads the tab you are acting on so it can tell what is there to click or type into. It reads that one tab, when you ask for something — not your browsing generally, and not in the background.

This means: the address, title and the names of things on a page you direct heybriz to are sent to our server, and from there to the model provider.

Model providers

The model that decides which action to take is Jev, from TypeSafe. It processes this content under its own terms. Speech recognition is a separate step, handled by Chrome and Google as described above — Jev never receives audio.

What we store on our servers

If you use a heybriz account, we store:

  • Account information — your email address and credit balance. Payments are processed by Creem, our merchant of record; we never see or store your card details.
  • Your requests and what heybriz did — the text of what you asked, the page address and the names of things on it that were sent with it, and the action it decided on. heybriz does not take screenshots; if a future version does, this policy will say so before that ships.
  • Usage records — timestamp, model name, token counts, and technical events about how each request went: how long it took, how many actions ran, and whether it failed.

Long runs of digits (such as account or ID numbers) are masked before anything is stored. We use this data to operate and secure the service, to debug failures, and to improve heybriz, including training and improving our own models.

What stays in your browser

Your sign-in token, your language choice and whether replies are read aloud are kept by the extension in Chrome's own storage, on your computer. They are not synced and not sent to us.

Credentials and sessions

heybriz acts on tabs you are already signed in to. It does not read, copy, or transmit your saved passwords, cookies, or session tokens.

Retention and deletion

We retain this data for as long as necessary to provide and improve our Services, and as required by applicable law. You can request deletion of your account and its records by contacting us at the address below.

Children

heybriz is for adults. Our Terms require you to be at least 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us data, write to hello@heybriz.com and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes, and will notify account holders of material changes by email.
