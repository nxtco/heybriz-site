WHAT YOU CAN SAY

One sentence, one action.

“Open YouTube”

Goes there. Name a site, or read out an address.

“Search for python tutorials”

Searches the site you name, or the web if you do not.

“Click the second video”

Finds it by what it says, not by where it sits.

“Type my email in the box”

Puts text where you mean it, and sends it when you say so.

“Scroll down”

The ordinary moves — up, down, top, bottom.

“Go back”

Back, forward, reload. Without reaching for the mouse.

It is not an agent that runs off on its own. You stay in the loop, one step at a time.

How it works

It shows its work before it moves.

You speak or type

Press the mic in the side panel and say it in plain language. The box below takes typing just the same.

heybriz says what it understood

Every step shows the action it picked, how sure it was, and what else it was considering.

It acts, or it asks

Sending, buying, deleting — it stops and waits for your yes. If two things could be what you meant, it asks instead of guessing.

Questions

The things people ask first

Do I need to install anything else?

No. It is a Chrome extension and it works on its own.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes — it runs in Chrome, so the computer underneath does not matter. You need Chrome 116 or newer.

Do I have to give you my passwords?

No. heybriz works inside the Chrome you are already signed into, so there is nothing to hand over. It never reads your saved passwords, cookies or session tokens — it just uses the tab the way you would.

What will it refuse to do?

Anything hard to undo stops and asks first. Signing, payment, transfers and deletion are blocked in code — not by asking the model nicely.

What happens to what I say?

Chrome turns your speech into text, which means the recording goes to Google’s speech service — the same as on any site that uses the browser’s microphone. heybriz never receives the audio. We get the text, the page address and the names of the things on the page, and send back one action.

How much does a command cost?

About one credit. $10 buys 10,000 credits, good for 12 months, and you get 1,000 free when you sign in.
