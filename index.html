Privacy by design

Your browser. Your accounts.
Your screen stays yours.

heybriz runs on your own PC and drives the browser you already use. Your files and the pages it reads for you are not kept on our servers, and it never touches your saved passwords, cookies or session tokens.

  • ✓ Signing, payment and deletion are refused in code — not by asking the model nicely
  • ✓ Account, card and ID numbers are masked before anything is stored
  • ✓ Your pages, files and screenshots are never stored on our servers
  • ✓ Chrome shows a banner the whole time it can act — you can stop it there
