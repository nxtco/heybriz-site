One sentence, one action. It is not an agent that runs off on its own — you stay in the loop, one step at a time.

It shows its work

You see what it understood

Every step shows the action it picked, how sure it was, and what else it considered.

It asks before anything hard to undo

Sending, buying, deleting, unsubscribing — it stops and waits for your yes.

It says when it is not sure

If two things on the page could be what you meant, it asks instead of guessing.

Your browser, your accounts

heybriz works inside the Chrome you are already signed into, so there is nothing to hand over. It never reads your saved passwords, cookies or session tokens — it uses the tab the way you would.

  • Signing, payment, transfers and deletion are refused by the software itself.
  • To understand a request it sends what you said, plus the address and the names of the links and boxes on the page — nothing else. That is the only server it talks to.
  • Chrome shows a banner the whole time it can act, and you can stop it there.

Questions

Do I need to install anything else?

No. It is a Chrome extension and it works on its own.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes — it runs in Chrome, so the computer underneath does not matter. Chrome 116 or newer.

Do I have to give you my passwords?

No. It acts in the tab you are already signed into and never reads your saved passwords, cookies or session tokens.

What will it refuse to do?

Anything hard to undo stops and asks first. Signing, payment, transfers and deletion are blocked in the software, not left to the model's judgement.

What happens to what I say?

Chrome turns your speech into text — it sends the recording to Google's speech service to do that, the same as any site that uses the browser's microphone. heybriz never receives the audio. We get the text, the page address and the names of the things on the page, and send back one action. There is no analytics.
