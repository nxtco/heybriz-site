How it works

Speak. Show. Delegate.

  1. SPEAK

    Say it — or type it

    Hold a key, say what you need. Dictation is transcribed on your own PC. It searches, reads the pages itself, and tells you where it got each part.

  2. SHOW

    Do a chore once, in front of it

    It works in the browser you're already in, and learns the task by watching you do it. It shows the plan before anything changes — payments and passwords are refused in code, not by asking nicely.

  3. DELEGATE

    From then on, it's one sentence

    “Do the weekly report.” The steps you taught it run again — in your browser, with your logins, while you watch.
