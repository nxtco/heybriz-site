Support

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Email hello@heybriz.com and we will answer within three business days. heybriz is in beta and a person reads every message.

What to tell us

Any of these that you know — none of them are required, they just save a round trip:

  • What you asked heybriz to do, and what happened instead.
  • The address of the page you were on.
  • The exact message you saw, if there was one.

The microphone does not work

Chrome asks for the microphone on an ordinary tab, not in the side panel, so the first time you press the mic heybriz opens a small page for that. Allow it there once and the panel listens from then on.

  • Nothing happened when you pressed it? That page may have opened in a background tab. Look for a tab titled heybriz — microphone.
  • You refused by accident? Open chrome://extensions, find heybriz, then Details → Site settings → Microphone and set it to Allow.
  • No microphone on this computer? The box below the mic still takes typed commands — everything works the same way.

It says it cannot act on this page

Chrome does not let extensions touch its own pages. On chrome:// pages, the Web Store, or a blank new tab, heybriz can still open a site for you ("open YouTube"), but it cannot click or type there. Go to an ordinary site and try again.

It asked instead of doing it

Two things make heybriz stop and ask. Either the action is hard to undo — sending, buying, deleting — or more than one thing on the page could have been what you meant. Open Why this on the step to see which, and what else it was considering.

heybriz stopped part-way

If you ran out of credits, the work stops where it was and says so. Top up, then tell heybriz to continue — it picks up from there rather than starting over.

If it stopped for another reason, the conversation keeps what it had already found. Say what you want it to do next.

Something it refused to do

Signing, payment, transfer and deletion are refused in the software itself, and some actions ask you before they run. That is by design — those stay yours to do. It is not a fault, and asking again will not change it.

Credits, billing and refunds

Your balance and history are on heybriz.com once you sign in. Payments are handled by Creem, our merchant of record, so their name appears on your statement rather than ours. Refunds are described in our Refund Policy — and if credits were spent because heybriz malfunctioned, tell us and we will make it right.

Your data

What we store, and what stays in your browser, is set out in our Privacy Policy. To have your account and its records deleted, write to us at the address above.
