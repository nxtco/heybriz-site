Support

Last updated: 2026-08-24

Email hello@heybriz.com and we will answer within three business days. heybriz is in beta and a person reads every message.

What to tell us

Any of these that you know — none of them are required, they just save a round trip:

  • What you asked heybriz to do, and what happened instead.
  • The version, from Settings in the app.
  • The exact message you saw, if there was one.

The extension says it cannot reach heybriz

The extension talks to the heybriz app over a connection inside your own computer, so both have to be running and paired.

  • Is the app open? The extension cannot do anything on its own. Start heybriz first.
  • Did you pair it? Open Settings in the app, copy the pairing code, and paste it into the extension's popup.
  • Is it the right extension? If you also run a development build, the code from one app will not work in the other extension. The message names which one it expects.

The pairing code was rejected

Codes are tied to one installation of the app, and a new code replaces the old one. Open Settings, generate a fresh code, and paste that. If you reloaded an unpacked extension, generate a new code as well — reloading can change the extension's identity, and the app only accepts the one it was paired with.

heybriz stopped part-way

If you ran out of credits, the work stops where it was and says so. Top up, then tell heybriz to continue — it picks up from there rather than starting over.

If it stopped for another reason, the conversation keeps what it had already found. Say what you want it to do next.

Something it refused to do

Signing, payment, transfer and deletion are refused in the software itself, and some actions ask you before they run. That is by design — those stay yours to do. It is not a fault, and asking again will not change it.

Credits, billing and refunds

Your balance and history are in Settings. Payments are handled by Creem, our merchant of record, so their name appears on your statement rather than ours. Refunds are described in our Refund Policy — and if credits were spent because heybriz malfunctioned, tell us and we will make it right.

Your data

What we store, and what stays on your computer, is set out in our Privacy Policy. To have your account and its records deleted, write to us at the address above.
